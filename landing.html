How to install

  1. Android: download the APK, open it, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser if asked, then tap Install.
  2. Windows: run Billaura-Setup.exe. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Any device: open in Chrome or Edge and choose Install app from the menu.

Questions

Things shopkeepers ask

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and is built for small shops. Pro lifts the limits and adds extras like branches and the online store.

What if the internet or electricity goes?

Billing works offline. You only need internet to sign in, sync to the cloud, check your licence and update. A phone or laptop on battery keeps selling.

Where is my data kept?

On your device first. If you sign in, a copy is also kept in your private cloud account, where only your shop can read it. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do updates work?

Browser and installed web app update automatically. The Windows app downloads in the background and installs when you close it. On Android you get an in-app notice; tap it and install over the old version, your data is kept.

Can I print a receipt on a thermal printer?

Yes. Bills can be printed on 58 mm and 80 mm receipt printers or A4, and shared as PDF or on WhatsApp.

Does it do FBR or PRA tax invoices?

Not yet. You can add sales tax to your bills today. Integration with FBR and PRA invoicing is planned; follow the in-app notices.

Android warns me when installing the APK.

That is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store. Allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser once, then it installs normally.

Windows says "unknown publisher".

The app is not yet code-signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
