Terms, Licence & Privacy

The rules, in plain language

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Applies to the Billaura web app, Android app, Windows app and the Billaura website.

The short version
  • Your shop data belongs to you. We do not sell it, show ads with it, or use it for tracking.
  • Billaura saves on your device first. Cloud sync is optional and private to your account.
  • The Free plan has no time limit. Pro is paid for by a manual bank or wallet transfer that we verify.
  • Billaura helps you keep accounts and print bills. You stay responsible for your tax filings and for checking your numbers.
  • If something goes wrong we are liable only up to what you paid us. Please keep your own backups.

2. What Billaura is

Billaura is a billing and shop-management tool. It lets you make sales bills, quotations, purchases, expenses and returns, track stock, customers, suppliers and udhaar balances, close your cash drawer at the end of the day, run reports and share documents by print, PDF or WhatsApp.

It is software, not an accountant, auditor, lawyer or tax adviser. Totals, profit figures, stock counts and tax amounts are calculated from what you enter. If you enter or import wrong data, the results will be wrong.

We may improve, rename, move or remove features. If we remove a feature that you pay for, we will tell you inside the app first and, where reasonable, give you a refund for the unused part (see Refunds).

3. Your account

  • Using without an account. You can use Billaura on one device without signing in. Your data then lives only on that device.
  • Signing in. With an account you can sync to the cloud, use more than one device and hold a licence. Keep your password private. Actions taken with your login are treated as yours.
  • Staff. You can add staff and give them roles. Staff PINs are stored on your device in a scrambled (hashed) form. You decide who gets access and what they may do. You are responsible for what your staff do in your shop's data, including discounts, refunds, cancelled bills and stock changes.
  • Owner PIN. Some actions can ask for the owner's PIN. If you lose it, we may not be able to recover it for you. Write it down somewhere safe.
  • One shop, one account. Do not share one account between unrelated businesses. Do not resell your login.

4. Licence and plans

We give you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use Billaura for your own business, as long as you follow these terms.

Free plan

Free has no expiry. It comes with usage limits that are shown in the app under Plans, such as how many items, parties or documents you can keep. Limits can change for new users; if we ever reduce limits for existing users we will give notice in the app first and will not delete data you already have.

Pro plans

A paid plan raises or removes the limits and may include extras such as branches and stock transfers, the online store page and priority support. What each plan includes, its price and its length are shown in the app when you choose it, and what is shown when you pay is what applies to that payment.

How the licence works

  • A licence is tied to your account and to a limited number of devices. The device limit for your plan is shown in the app. If you reach the limit, you can remove an old device from your account to free a place.
  • The app checks your licence when you are online. It then keeps working offline for a grace period (currently about ten days) before it needs to check again.
  • If a paid plan ends, is cancelled or cannot be verified after the grace period, Billaura goes back to Free-plan limits. Your data is not deleted and is not locked. You can still open it, print it and export it. Features above the Free limits stop accepting new entries until you renew or reduce your data to fit.
  • Limits are enforced by the app and its licence checks. Trying to get around them (for example by editing the app, faking a licence or sharing a licence across unrelated shops) breaks these terms and can end your licence without refund.

You may not copy, resell, rent or white-label Billaura, remove our branding, reverse engineer it beyond what the law allows, or use it to build a competing product. Source code that is shown or shared publicly remains ours unless a separate open-source licence says otherwise in that file.

  • Feature removed: if we remove a paid feature, you get a refund for the unused part of your plan.
  • Otherwise: plans are not refundable after the 7 days because the service has been provided, but you keep the plan until its end date. A change of mind, a shop that closed, or not using the app is not a reason for refund after 7 days.

7. Your shop data

  • It is yours. Everything you enter (items, customers, bills, prices, photos, staff names) belongs to you. We take no ownership of it. You give us only the permission needed to store it, sync it and show it back to you.
  • You are the one responsible for it. That includes having the right to store your customers' names and phone numbers, and keeping bills as long as the law requires you to.
  • Backups are your safety net. The app keeps automatic restore points on your device and can save a backup file. A phone that is lost, reset or has its browser data cleared can lose data that was never synced or backed up. We strongly suggest saving a backup file regularly and keeping it somewhere other than the same phone.
  • Export. You can export your data at any time, including after a plan ends.
  • Recycle Bin. Cancelled bills go to a Recycle Bin on your device. It keeps the most recent 200 items and is cleared if you clear your browser data.

  • If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your cloud data and profile within 30 days, except payment records we must keep. Data copies on your own devices and in backup files you made stay with you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you by emailing us. You can also use the app without signing in. Keep your login and PINs private and tell us quickly if you think someone else has access.

Security

We use sign-in controls, per-shop access rules and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, and your data is also only as safe as your phone, your password and the people you give access to. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you without unreasonable delay.
